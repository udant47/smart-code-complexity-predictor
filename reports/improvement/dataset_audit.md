## 2. Label consistency

- Distinct feature vectors: 4,402; rows whose identical vector carries a different label: **0**.
- Near-duplicate clusters: 104 (223 rows); clusters with different labels: **0**.
- No evidence of label noise was found with these checks, so no labels were changed or removed.

## 3. Leakage

- Problems in more than one partition: **0**.
- Near-duplicate clusters in more than one partition: **0**.
- Split: StratifiedGroupKFold, groups = problem merged with near-duplicate clusters.

## 4. Diversity

- Programs per problem: median 11, max 135.
- 39 problems have solutions with different labels; 95.6% of programs share their problem's majority label.
  The label is mostly a property of the *problem*, so generalising to unseen problems is the real difficulty.
- 30 algorithm tags; most common: implementation (1638), math (1598), dp (1276), greedy (1271), brute force (902), bitmasks (624), sortings (603), binary search (576), constructive algorithms (358), graphs (339).
